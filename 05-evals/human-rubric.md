# Human Evaluation Rubric · Juno (Revised)

## What graders score

* **What you’re scoring:** Juno Prototype Tool
* **Value proposition:** Create different ranked options as clickable prototypes that demonstrate new feature possibilities.

---

## Dimensions

| Dimension | Question | Scale | 1 (Poor / Missing) | 3 (Moderate / Partial) | 5 (Excellent / Clear) |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Citations & Evidence** | Is each feature option backed by valid supporting evidence? | 1–5 | No evidence or citations provided. | Relies on anecdotal internal assumptions or weak signals without data. | Backed by clear external or internal data (e.g., user interview quotes, survey results, support ticket metrics, or analytics). |
| **Specific Outcome** | Does each feature option deliver a clear, measurable user outcome? | 1–5 | Outcome is vague, overly broad, or multi-directional to the point of confusion. | Outcome is stated, but success criteria or user benefit are ambiguous. | Delivers a single, highly distinct, and measurable outcome for the user. |
| **Differentiation & Vision** | Does the feature option offer a differentiated approach compared to baseline? | 1–5 | Rehashes existing internal ideas with no new strategic angle or value. | Offers a minor iteration on existing solutions. | Introduces a distinct, creative feature approach or workflow model that moves beyond current product baselines. |
| **Comprehension** | Is the feature option clear and intuitive for the user to understand? | 1–5 | The prototype or concept is unclear, causing immediate user confusion. | Requires excessive explanation or guidance to grasp the utility. | The concept is immediately intuitive and easily understood by the user. |

---

## Calibration & Process

* **Sample Size:** 5 feature options evaluated per cycle.
* **Evaluator Pool:** 1 Product Manager, 1 UX Designer, and the primary author.
* **Dispute Resolution:** If scores diverge across evaluators, the feature option is reviewed by a secondary Product Manager and escalated to the Chief Product Officer (CPO) if necessary.

---

## Pass Bar

* **Minimum Passing Score:** An average score of **4.0 or higher** across all dimensions, with no individual dimension scoring a 1.

### Automatic Disqualifiers (Outright Fails)
Any feature option fails immediately, regardless of its overall average, if it triggers any of the following criteria:
* **Unclear Outcome:** Fails to define a concrete, specific benefit for the user.
* **Comprehension Failure:** Scores a 1 in Comprehension (causes severe confusion).
